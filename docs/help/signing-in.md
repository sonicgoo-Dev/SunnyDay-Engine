# Signing in

SunnyDay is by invitation for now.

1. Open **app.sunnydaycrm.com**.
2. Tap **Sign in with Google**.
3. Choose the Google account your invitation was sent to.

That is all. You stay signed in on that phone or computer for 30 days from
the last time you used it.

**"SunnyDay is by invitation for now."** You signed in with a Google
account that has not been invited. Tap **Try a different account** and choose
the right one. If you only have one, ask the person who invited you to add
that address.

**Signing out.** Tap **Sign out**, beside your name at the bottom of the menu. Do this on a
shared computer.
