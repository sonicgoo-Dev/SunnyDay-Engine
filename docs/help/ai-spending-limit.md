# The monthly AI spending limit

Your agents cost a little each time they read or draft something. The
limit is the most they may spend in a month. It protects you from a
surprise bill.

## Seeing and changing it

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Under **AI spending** you see what has been used this month.
3. To change the limit, type a whole number of dollars from 0 to 200 and
   tap **Save limit**.

## At the limit

Your agents pause, and Settings says so. Nothing is lost: everything
already drafted stays where it is. They start again when you raise the
limit, or on the 1st of the next month.

## Switching the agents off

Set the limit to **0**.
