# Connecting your mail

SunnyDay reads your mail so it can catch new leads and keep each person's
story up to date. It only reads. Nothing in your mailbox is ever marked as
read, moved, labelled or deleted.

## Your main mailbox (Gmail)

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Under **Your main mailbox**, tap **Connect Gmail**.
3. Choose your work Gmail account.
4. Google may say **"Google hasn't verified this app."** That is expected
   while SunnyDay is in testing. Tap **Advanced**, then **Go to SunnyDay**.
5. Google lists what SunnyDay is asking for. **Tick every box**, then tap
   **Continue**. If a box is left unticked, SunnyDay will ask you to try again.

You are back in Settings, which shows your address and **Connected**. The
first read takes a few minutes.

SunnyDay asks for permission to read your mail, and to write and send
messages. Reading is all it does today. Nothing is ever sent unless you
approve that message first.

It also asks to see your Google Contacts, for Build my list. It can only
read them, never change them. If you connected Gmail before this was
asked for, tap **Include my Google Contacts** in Settings.

## A second mailbox

For an older address you still get mail at. SunnyDay reads it, both what
you receive there and what you send from it, and never sends from it
itself.

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Under **A second mailbox**, type the **email address** and its **password**.
3. The **Mail server** box fills in by itself. Leave it, unless your mail
   provider gave you a different one.
4. Tap **Connect this mailbox**.

The password is stored sealed and used only to read that mailbox.

**"The mail server did not accept that address and password."** Check both
for typing mistakes. It is the same password you use to read that mail on
the web.

## "Needs reconnecting"

The mailbox stopped accepting SunnyDay, usually because its password
changed or the permission was removed. For Gmail, tap **Connect again**.
For a second mailbox, tap **Disconnect**, then connect it again.

## Disconnecting

Tap **Disconnect**, then **Yes, disconnect**. SunnyDay stops reading that
mailbox and forgets what it read from it. Your mailbox itself is not touched.
