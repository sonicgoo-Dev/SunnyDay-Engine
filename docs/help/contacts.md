# Contacts and the contact page

Everyone you work with is in **Contacts**, and each person has their own
page holding everything you know about them.

## The three tabs

- **Clients**: anyone past Talking, including past clients.
- **Leads**: people at New, Written to or Talking.
- **Business contacts**: lenders, title, inspectors, other agents and
  referral partners.

Where someone sits follows their stage, so moving a lead past Talking
makes them a client. People you have marked Not now, Not a lead, Gone
quiet or Lost are folded away under **Not active** at the bottom of Leads.

## Adding someone

1. Tap **Add** at the top of any screen.
2. Type their name, and a phone or email if you have one.
3. Tap **Buyer**, **Seller** or both. Their stage starts at New; change
   it if they are further along.
4. Choose **How did they find you?** It is asked now because it is hard
   to remember later. "Don't remember" is a fine answer.
5. For a couple, tap **Add their partner too** and type the partner's
   name. They share one household, one stage and the same answer to how
   they found you.
6. Tap **Save**.

For a lender or another agent, choose **A business contact** at the top.

## The contact page

Every part has an **Edit** button and every part is optional: phones and
emails, how to reach them, household, dates, personal notes, what they
want, how they found you, and your own fields.

- **The name they go by** is what messages to them will use.
- **Your private nickname** is for your memory only. It shows on their
  page and in search, and never in anything they could see.
- **Call** and **Text** open your phone. **Email** opens Gmail as your
  main address. If they asked not to be texted or emailed, that button is
  greyed out.

## Moving a stage

Choose the new stage from the list. Moving someone forward happens at
once. Moving them back asks first ("Move back to Talking?"), and their
story records it.

## Household

**Link a partner** joins two people already in your contacts into one
household. **Add child or family** adds someone who is not a contact
themselves, with a birthday if you know it. Removing one asks first,
because they come off every page in the household.

**Leave household** takes one person out and keeps the others together.
Someone who is alone in a household has nothing to leave.

## Your own fields

Tap **Add a field** to track anything else, such as "holiday card list".
A field you add shows on every contact.

## Searching

Type any detail in the search box: a name, a phone number, a street, a
child, a dog. A pet noted on one partner's page finds them both.
Search shows the first 100 people it finds; add another word to narrow
it down. Your private nickname for someone finds them too.

## Their story

Each change, each note you add, and the emails SunnyDay has read to or
from their address appear newest first. Calls and texts from your phone
do not appear; SunnyDay cannot see your phone.

## Putting someone away

**Put away** at the bottom of a page hides that person from your lists
and search. Nothing is deleted. To bring them back, tap **People you have
put away** at the bottom of Contacts, open them, and tap **Bring back**.
