# Putting SunnyDay on your phone's home screen

SunnyDay runs in your phone's browser. Adding it to the home screen gives
it its own icon and opens it full screen, like an app.

## iPhone

1. Open **app.sunnydaycrm.com** in **Safari** and sign in.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add**.

## Android

1. Open **app.sunnydaycrm.com** in **Chrome** and sign in.
2. Tap the **three dots** at the top right.
3. Tap **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**).
4. Tap **Add**.

Open SunnyDay from the new icon from now on.
