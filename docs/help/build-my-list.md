# Build my list

Build my list finds the people in your mail and asks you about each one.
Nobody is added to your contacts until you say Yes.

It is the card at the top of **Contacts**.

## How it works

1. Tap **Start with the last 12 months**. SunnyDay reads one year of mail.
   This takes a while; you can close SunnyDay and come back.
   It reads every mailbox you have connected: what you received and what
   you sent, from your main mailbox and from your second one.
2. It sets aside newsletters, notices and other machine mail by itself.
3. For each likely person, it reads a few of your messages with them and
   suggests what they are to you: past client, client now, lead or
   business contact.
4. The card says how many people are ready. Tap **Look at them**.

## Going through the cards

You see ten people at a time. Each card says who they are, what SunnyDay
thinks they are, and one line on why.

- **Yes** adds them as suggested.
- **Change** lets you fix the name, the kind, buyer or seller, the stage,
  how they found you, and which details to keep.
- **No** leaves them out. They go to **Not contacts**.
- **Yes to all** adds every card on the page that is ready to add. A card
  that still needs something from you stays for you to answer.

After ten, choose **Keep going** or **That's enough for today**. Your
place is kept.

A person you add as a client now needs a stage from you. SunnyDay never
guesses how far along a deal is.

## How they found you

SunnyDay fills this in only when a message plainly says it. Otherwise it
puts **Don't remember**. You can change it on the card, or later on the
person's page.

## Going further back

When a year is finished, the card offers **Go back another year**. One
year at a time, up to five years. Nothing further back is read unless
you tap.

## Your Google Contacts

If you allowed it when you connected Gmail, SunnyDay also reads your
Google Contacts, to fill in names and phone numbers. It can never change
them. People in your Google Contacts who did not turn up in your mail are
listed under **From your contacts**, where you tick the ones who belong.

Did not allow it the first time? Open **Settings** and tap **Include my
Google Contacts**.

## Contacts from your phone

On an iPhone:

1. Open the **Contacts** app.
2. Tap **Lists** at the top left.
3. Press and hold **All Contacts**, then tap **Export**.
4. Tap **Save to Files** and save it.
5. In SunnyDay, open **Contacts**, tap **Add contacts from your phone**,
   and choose that file.

Photos are taken out before the file leaves your phone. SunnyDay keeps
the people and throws the file away.

## Not contacts

Everyone SunnyDay set aside, and everyone you said No to. If someone is
there by mistake, tap **Move to my list** and they come back as a card.

## Where a detail came from

On a person's page, each detail added this way says where it came from:
"from an email on Mar 3, 2025", "from Google Contacts" or "from your
phone contacts".

## What it costs

Reading a year of mail usually costs one to two dollars of your monthly
AI spending limit. That is an estimate. If the limit is reached, Build my
list pauses and says so. Everything found so far is kept, and nobody is
paid for twice. It carries on when you raise the limit in **Settings**, or
on the 1st.

## What the AI sees

For each likely person, SunnyDay sends up to eight of your messages with
them to Claude, the AI made by Anthropic, and asks one question: who is
this person to you, and which contact details do the messages show.

- SunnyDay does not keep the text of those messages. It keeps the
  suggestion, and a short top line of each message.
- Machine mail is never sent to the AI.
- Anthropic's terms for business use say: "Anthropic may not train models
  on Customer Content from Services."
  ([Commercial Terms, section B](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/commercial-terms))
- Anthropic says it deletes what it was sent within 30 days.
  ([How long is data stored](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/7996866-how-long-do-you-store-my-organization-s-data))
- SunnyDay trains nothing on your mail or your clients.

## Your mailbox is not touched

Build my list only reads. Nothing in your mailbox or your Google Contacts
is marked, moved, changed or deleted.
